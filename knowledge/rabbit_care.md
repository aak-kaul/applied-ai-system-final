# Rabbit care guidelines

General, widely published husbandry guidance condensed for this project. Rabbits are sensitive to diet and stress, so check details with a vet who treats rabbits.

## Rabbit feeding
applies: species=rabbit
task: key=hay | category=feeding | title=Refresh hay, greens and water | duration=10 | priority=high | frequency=Daily | windows=07:00-09:00,17:30-19:00
Fresh grass hay should make up most of a rabbit's diet and be available all the time, with a daily portion of fresh leafy greens and only a small measured amount of pellets. Young rabbits have different needs, so confirm with a vet.

## Rabbit exercise
applies: species=rabbit
task: key=play | category=play | title=Supervised exercise time out of the hutch | duration=60 | priority=high | frequency=Daily | windows=17:00-20:00
Rabbits need space to run, jump and explore for several hours a day. This plan books one supervised hour as a minimum, so also give them a safe exercise pen or room to use.

## Rabbit litter and cage cleaning
applies: species=rabbit
task: key=litter | category=other | title=Spot-clean litter box | duration=10 | priority=high | frequency=Daily | windows=08:00-09:30
task: key=litterdeep | category=other | title=Full cage and litter clean | duration=30 | priority=low | frequency=Weekly | windows=10:00-12:00
Spot-clean the litter box every day and do a full clean of the cage and litter every week to keep the rabbit healthy and the home fresh.

## Rabbit grooming
applies: species=rabbit
task: key=brush | category=grooming | title=Brush coat (extra when moulting) | duration=10 | priority=low | frequency=Weekly | windows=10:00-12:00
Rabbits groom themselves, but weekly brushing helps, and more often while they moult, because swallowed fur can cause blockages.
