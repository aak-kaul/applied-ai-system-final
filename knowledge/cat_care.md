# Cat care guidelines

General, widely published husbandry guidance condensed for this project. It is not veterinary advice: your vet's instructions always come first.

## Kitten feeding
applies: species=cat; stage=baby
task: key=meal | category=feeding | title=Kitten meal | duration=10 | priority=high | frequency=Daily | windows=07:00-08:30,12:00-13:30,17:30-19:00
Kittens grow fast and need three or four small meals a day of food made for kittens. Follow the food label for portions and keep meal times regular.

## Adult cat feeding
applies: species=cat; stage=adult,senior
task: key=meal | category=feeding | title=Cat meal | duration=10 | priority=high | frequency=Daily | windows=07:00-08:30,17:30-19:00
Most adult cats do well on two measured meals a day. Older cats may need different portions or food, so check with your vet.

## Kitten play
applies: species=cat; stage=baby
task: key=play | category=play | title=Interactive play session | duration=10 | priority=high | frequency=Daily | windows=09:00-10:30,14:00-15:30,19:00-20:30
Kittens have bursts of energy and learn through play. Several short sessions a day with wand toys and balls are better than one long one.

## Adult cat play
applies: species=cat; stage=adult
task: key=play | category=play | title=Interactive play session | duration=15 | priority=medium | frequency=Daily | windows=08:30-09:30,18:30-20:00
Ten to fifteen minutes of interactive play once or twice a day with a wand toy keeps an adult cat active and satisfies hunting instincts.

## Senior cat play
applies: species=cat; stage=senior
task: key=play | category=play | title=Gentle play session | duration=10 | priority=medium | frequency=Daily | windows=18:30-20:00
Older cats still enjoy play but tire sooner. Short gentle sessions with low toys keep them moving.

## Cat litter box
applies: species=cat
task: key=litter | category=other | title=Scoop litter box | duration=10 | priority=high | frequency=Daily | windows=08:30-10:00
task: key=litterdeep | category=other | title=Deep clean litter box | duration=20 | priority=low | frequency=Weekly | windows=10:00-12:00
Scoop the litter box at least once a day, because cats avoid a dirty box. Empty and wash it with mild soap regularly, about once a week.

## Cat water
applies: species=cat
task: key=water | category=other | title=Refresh water bowl | duration=5 | priority=medium | frequency=Daily | windows=07:00-09:00
Fresh clean water should always be available. Wash the bowl and refill it each day.

## Cat grooming
applies: species=cat
task: key=brush | category=grooming | title=Brush coat | duration=10 | priority=low | frequency=Weekly | windows=10:00-12:00
Weekly brushing removes loose hair, reduces hairballs and lets you check skin and nails.

## Long-haired cat brushing
applies: species=cat; condition=long-haired
task: key=brush | category=grooming | title=Daily brushing (long coat) | duration=10 | priority=medium | frequency=Daily | windows=17:00-18:30
Long-haired cats mat quickly, so brushing every day keeps the coat tangle-free.
