# Dog care guidelines

General, widely published husbandry guidance condensed for this project. It is not veterinary advice: breed, health and your vet's instructions always come first.

## Puppy feeding
applies: species=dog; stage=baby
task: key=meal | category=feeding | title=Puppy meal | duration=15 | priority=high | frequency=Daily | windows=07:00-08:30,12:00-13:30,17:30-19:00
Puppies under about six months have small stomachs and high energy needs, so they do best on three or four small meals a day rather than one or two large ones. Feed at regular times and follow the food label for portion size.

## Puppy potty and walk breaks
applies: species=dog; stage=baby
task: key=walk | category=walk | title=Short walk and potty break | duration=15 | priority=high | frequency=Daily | windows=08:30-10:00,14:00-15:30,19:00-20:30
Puppies need frequent, short trips outside, especially after waking, after meals and after play. Keep walks short and let them sniff and toilet; long hard exercise is not good for growing puppies.

## Puppy play and training
applies: species=dog; stage=baby
task: key=play | category=play | title=Play and short training session | duration=20 | priority=medium | frequency=Daily | windows=10:30-12:00,16:00-17:30
Short play and training sessions teach a puppy good habits and burn off energy. Several brief sessions a day work better than one long one.

## Puppy grooming and handling
applies: species=dog; stage=baby
task: key=brush | category=grooming | title=Gentle brushing and paw handling | duration=10 | priority=low | frequency=Weekly | windows=16:00-18:00
Getting a puppy used to gentle brushing, touching paws and checking ears makes later grooming and vet visits easier. Keep sessions calm and short.

## Puppy water
applies: species=dog; stage=baby
task: key=water | category=other | title=Refresh water bowl | duration=5 | priority=medium | frequency=Daily | windows=07:00-09:00
Fresh clean water should always be available. Wash the bowl and refill it each day.

## Adult dog feeding
applies: species=dog; stage=adult
task: key=meal | category=feeding | title=Adult dog meal | duration=15 | priority=high | frequency=Daily | windows=07:00-08:30,17:30-19:00
Most healthy adult dogs do well on two meals a day, roughly twelve hours apart, with portions set by the food label and your vet.

## Adult dog walks
applies: species=dog; stage=adult
task: key=walk | category=walk | title=Walk | duration=30 | priority=high | frequency=Daily | windows=07:00-09:00,17:00-19:00
Most adult dogs need at least thirty minutes to an hour of exercise every day, often split into a morning and an evening walk. Needs vary with breed, size and energy.

## Adult dog play
applies: species=dog; stage=adult
task: key=play | category=play | title=Play or fetch session | duration=20 | priority=medium | frequency=Daily | windows=18:30-20:00
Active play, fetch and puzzle toys keep an adult dog mentally stimulated and help prevent boredom behaviours.

## Adult dog grooming and baths
applies: species=dog; stage=adult
task: key=brush | category=grooming | title=Brush coat and check ears and nails | duration=15 | priority=low | frequency=Weekly | windows=10:00-12:00
task: key=bath | category=grooming | title=Bath day (repeat every four to eight weeks if needed) | duration=30 | priority=low | frequency=Once | windows=12:00-14:00
Brushing once or twice a week removes loose hair, and a quick check of ears, nails and skin catches problems early. Many dogs only need a bath every four to eight weeks, depending on coat and lifestyle.

## Adult dog water
applies: species=dog; stage=adult,senior
task: key=water | category=other | title=Refresh water bowl | duration=5 | priority=medium | frequency=Daily | windows=07:00-09:00
Fresh clean water should always be available. Wash the bowl and refill it each day.

## Senior dog feeding
applies: species=dog; stage=senior
task: key=meal | category=feeding | title=Senior dog meal | duration=15 | priority=high | frequency=Daily | windows=07:00-08:30,17:30-19:00
Older dogs usually keep two regular meals a day. Appetite changes, weight loss or trouble chewing in an older dog are worth raising with a vet.

## Senior dog walks
applies: species=dog; stage=senior
task: key=walk | category=walk | title=Gentle walk | duration=20 | priority=high | frequency=Daily | windows=08:30-10:00,13:00-14:30,17:00-18:30
Senior dogs usually do better with shorter, gentler, more frequent walks than one long outing, which is easier on stiff joints.

## Senior dog play
applies: species=dog; stage=senior
task: key=play | category=play | title=Gentle play and enrichment | duration=15 | priority=medium | frequency=Daily | windows=15:00-16:30
Calm play, sniffing games and food puzzles keep an older dog engaged without straining joints.

## Senior dog grooming and baths
applies: species=dog; stage=senior
task: key=brush | category=grooming | title=Brush coat and check skin, lumps and nails | duration=15 | priority=low | frequency=Weekly | windows=10:00-12:00
task: key=bath | category=grooming | title=Bath day (repeat every four to eight weeks if needed) | duration=30 | priority=low | frequency=Once | windows=12:00-14:00
Gentle weekly brushing is a good moment to check older skin for lumps, sore spots and overgrown nails. Many dogs only need a bath every four to eight weeks.

## Long-haired dog brushing
applies: species=dog; condition=long-haired
task: key=brush | category=grooming | title=Daily brushing (long coat) | duration=15 | priority=medium | frequency=Daily | windows=18:00-19:30
Long or thick coats tangle and mat quickly, so brushing every day keeps the coat comfortable and the skin healthy.
