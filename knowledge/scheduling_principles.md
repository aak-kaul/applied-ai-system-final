# Scheduling principles

Rules of thumb the planner uses to check its own draft schedule.

## Space meals apart
applies: species=any
check: key=meal_spacing | min_gap=180
Leave a few hours between meals so the pet is hungry at mealtimes and digestion is not rushed. The planner checks that scheduled meals have at least three hours between the end of one meal and the start of the next.

## Keep exercise away from mealtimes
applies: species=dog
check: key=exercise_after_meal | gap=60 | min_duration=20
Avoid longer walks and energetic play for about an hour after a meal, especially for large deep-chested dogs, to lower the risk of stomach upset or bloat. Short potty breaks are fine.

## Consistency matters
applies: species=any
Pets settle best with a predictable routine, so keep meals, walks and play at roughly the same times each day.

## Share care across people
applies: species=any
Give each task to a person who is actually free at that time, and avoid booking one person for two things at once.
